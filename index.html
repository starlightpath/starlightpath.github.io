About the Game

Starlight Path is a cozy 2D pixel-art adventure RPG. You play a lost traveler who arrives in a forest guarded by starlight. As night falls, you explore the woods, collect items, complete small quests, and slowly uncover the secret behind the dimming stars.

Features

Night Exploration

A moonlit forest with lantern light, glowing fireflies, and soft ambient color.

Heartfelt Quests

Help a worried bunny, a busy blacksmith, and a little girl who lost her light.

Dialogue That Changes

Characters remember what you have done, and their words change as the story moves on.

Guided Start

A gentle objective guide shows you what to do next, so you are never lost for long.

Save Anywhere

Save with F5 and continue later from the exact place you stopped.

Three Languages

Play in English, Traditional Chinese, or Japanese. Switch at any time from the title screen.

Beta 1.0.8 — What's New

Five Save Slots

Choose from five independent saves. Your progress lives beside the Windows EXE in game_save_data/slot_1.json through slot_5.json.

Achievements

Discover eight achievements as you help friends, explore and listen to the forest's stories.

More Story

A mysterious letter, the elder's wisdom, and the mayor's legend of five wishes add new moments to the adventure.

Refined Menus & English Default

A clearer save-slot menu and achievement gallery. New installations start in English; existing language preferences remain saved.

Save slots are part of the downloadable Godot game, not playable in the browser. Beta features should be tested before release.

Continue Game & Settings

✦ Continue Game

Choose Continue Game on the title screen to resume an existing adventure. You must have saved a game first.

Save folder (planned for the Windows build):

game_save_data/

Keep this folder beside the game executable when moving or backing up the game. The exact location depends on the installed game build.
